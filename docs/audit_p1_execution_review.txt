Accounting and causality evidence
1. Derivative principal does not leave cash. Existing quantity times mark change
   is recognized once; new delta quantity times mark-minus-fill enters cash at
   execution. Fees use absolute filled notional; adverse slippage has the correct
   sign for buys, sells, reversals and closures. Synthetic reconciliation verifies
   NAV change = price PnL + execution basis PnL + funding - fees - slippage.
2. Forecast funding estimates use only events whose available_at is strictly
   before execution minus one hour. They extrapolate a historical event-rate mean
   with historical median event spacing, rather than using future actual rates.
   Changing a not-yet-available event leaves the first two allocator forecasts
   unchanged while changing the ledger's actual payment at the event boundary.
3. Payments use actual supplied funding_time/rate events and the pre-rebalance
   position. Positive rates charge longs. Boundary events precede rebalancing;
   intrahour events use the latest observed hourly mark as an explicit proxy.
   No periodic funding schedule is invented. Missing source events remain a
   source completeness concern, not something this ledger can infer.
4. Evaluator mark prices are the mark-kline open at the hourly boundary; current
   or future hourly close/high/low is not used as that boundary's mark. This is
   an assumed observable boundary proxy, not a historical feed-arrival proof.
5. Covariance uses strictly earlier hourly open-price returns. Perturbing all
   current/future execution opens leaves the initial covariance unchanged.
   Frozen 12-hour prediction and covariance inputs match PortfolioConfig's
   declared 12-hour objective horizon. Covariance shrinkage and PSD projection
   are disclosed; horizon scaling assumes linear mean and variance scaling.
6. Optimizer turnover compares target exposures with actual quantity/execution
   price/current NAV weights after previous mark PnL and funding. Gross and
   single-name constraints govern pre-trade targets. Execution costs, market
   drift and mark/execution basis can increase actual exposure ratios beyond
   those targets; this is disclosed, not a strict realized exposure guarantee.
7. The bare ledger intentionally leaves final positions open. The evaluator
   explicitly sets a zero target at its final observed boundary and pays closing
   fees/slippage. In P1 no lot/minimum-notional restrictions are passed, so final
   quantities are zero. Generic ledger minimum-notional rules may retain dust,
   as documented in execution_contract.txt.
8. Solver errors/invalid solutions yield cash_fallback, with zero targets.
   Evaluator records the count and confirmation vetoes promotion when candidate
   solver failures occur. A new proof checks that failure after opening closes
   actual existing quantity, pays costs and still pays funding held at the
   failure boundary. No phantom reset to cash occurs.
9. Hourly execution bars are continuous, including bars with no signal. Missing
   signals go flat; they are not deleted from the return timeline. Missing hourly
   market prices cause a fail-closed error. Synthetic tests verify both behaviors.
10. Risk statistics use consecutive marked portfolio NAV changes, not prediction
    labels, asset rows or overlapping 12-hour outcomes. Initial execution costs
    remain in the return path and drawdown baseline. Total return compounds to
    final NAV / initial NAV - 1. Paired bootstrap resamples identical contiguous
    disjoint time blocks for candidate and reference; trailing incomplete blocks
    and minimum-block insufficiency are explicit.

Semantic limits for the P1 report
- Prediction labels are 12-hour entry-to-exit open-price simple returns.
  The policy reoptimizes hourly and evaluates marked hourly NAV returns. Label
  horizon is not a fixed holding-period guarantee; cashflows, costs, and mark
  basis separate actual strategy NAV from forecast label accuracy.
- Segment masks require label_end before the segment endpoint. Thus signal
  coverage ends approximately 12 hours before each segment ends. The evaluator
  then moves to cash and preserves every remaining hourly bar through its final
  boundary. This is deterministic endpoint handling and should be reported.
- risk_metrics counts the first boundary return (instant initial execution costs
  or zero) as one observation alongside subsequent hourly returns. The annualized
  metrics therefore use an observation convention rather than exact elapsed time
  between first and last timestamp. This is a small boundary effect for monthly
  segments and should not be interpreted as exact clock-time annualization.
- Stable historical funding extrapolation and open-mark observability are
  research assumptions. The ledger is not a full exchange liquidation, margin,
  intrahour mark, liquidity, delisting or order-book model. Maintenance breaches
  raise an error rather than fabricate a completed liquidation path.
- Seven-day confirmation has fewer than the frozen 20 required 48-hour blocks;
  promotion remains insufficient_sample. Exploratory aggregate paired intervals
  do not correct repeated architecture searches or establish prospective evidence.

These are synthetic accounting and semantic proofs. This reviewer ran no market
